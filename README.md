THE CITY WITHOUT SOULS A mystery between life and death Chapter 1 — The Awakening Sofia opened her eyes slowly. He was in the back seat of his parents' car, where he had fallen asleep during the trip. Outside, the city was plunged into darkness. He looked ahead. The benches were empty. The car doors, open. — Mother? Father? Desperate, she left and began to seek help. He walked through deserted streets, passed abandoned shops and called out to someone. There was no one. No car, no noise. It was as if the whole city had disappeared. Then, your cell phone vibrated. A message from the mother appeared on the screen: “Daughter, don't trust anyone... not even me.” Sofia felt a shiver. Before she could answer, she heard a voice behind her: — Finally you woke up. She turned around. There was no one. Chapter 2 — The Man in the Window Sofia entered a store to hide. A radio on the counter broadcast a message: — If you can hear this, don't look out the windows after midnight. And never respond when someone calls your name. Suddenly, he heard his father's voice: — Sofia! I'm in the square! Come get me! Through the window, he saw a man who looked like him, standing on the sidewalk. He smiled, but his eyes were completely black. The man started banging on the glass. — Daughter, open the door! Sofia hid until the beating stopped. When he looked again, he was gone. Your cell phone vibrated. “Do you want to find your parents? Go to the square before dawn.” Chapter 3 — The Lantern Girl In the square, Sofia found Helena, a girl holding a lantern. — Did you wake up in a car too? — Helena asked. Sofia nodded. — I've been stuck here for three years. This city is not what it seems. Helena took her to an abandoned hospital. In the basement, they found a room full of monitors. On one of the screens, Sofia recognized her parents, motionless inside the car. — We need to wake them up! — First, you need to find out the truth — Helena replied. On a table, Sofia found a recorder and pressed the button. His father's voice came out: — If you're listening to this, the accident happened. It was raining heavily. We lost control of the car... Then he heard the mother crying: — We managed to get out, but Sofia wouldn't wake up. When we got back to get her, it was too late... Sofia started crying. He remembered the headlights, the breaking glass and the mother's voice calling his name. She understood that she was trapped in a dream as her body struggled to survive. — Am I dead? — he asked. Helena lowered her head. — Not yet. But you have to choose if you want to go back. Chapter 4 — The Choice At the end of the corridor, a door shone brightly. On the other side, Sofia heard her mother: — Daughter, come home! She ran towards the door, but stopped when she realized that Helena remained motionless. — Aren't you coming with me? Helena smiled sadly. — I've already made my choice. Sofia walked through the door. Woke up in a hospital, surrounded by doctors. His mother held his hand, crying, while his father watched at the bedside. She had spent weeks in a coma after the accident. Against all expectations, it was alive. Months later, Sofia returned to her routine, but she never forgot the empty city. Until one night your cell phone vibrated. A message from an unknown number appeared: “Thanks for letting me out.” Sofia froze. Another message arrived: “Now it's your turn to meet me.” She ran to the window. Across the street, Helena held a flashlight. Behind her, dozens of people smiled silently. The clock struck 00:13. All the lights in the house went out. And a voice whispered in his ear: — You should never have come back. END... OR THE BEGINNING OF ANOTHER NIGHTMARE
